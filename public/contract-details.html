Notes from the contract

Scope of work

The items this contract covers, by name. Listing one changes no figure.

What's this

A fixed-rate contract prices the work as a whole, so a scope item carries no price of its own. Its job is to be named by a debit: "which item was this spend for".

Allowances

A cap on a named item. Plannr shows where you stand and settles nothing.

What's this

A cap is either a rupee amount or a ceiling per square foot. Its running spend is counted from the Money Debited entries you tag with it — real spend, never a figure you type.

No figure here touches Owed or Total contract. What an overrun or underrun does to the next payment is between you and the contractor.
