# Plannr 1.0.0 — release notes

**Status: draft.** The download is not out yet. These notes are for the first public release.

## What Plannr is

Plannr helps you keep track of the money you spend building your home. It is an Android app and
it works fully offline.

## What is in 1.0.0

This is the first public release. You can:

- write down the money you pay out and the money you receive;
- see how much you still owe your builder under the contract;
- sort your spending into groups, like materials and labour;
- set a budget and see how much of it is left;
- see where the money went in a chart, and share it as a PDF;
- fix mistakes: deleted items go to a recycle bin first;
- save a backup file at any time, and lock it with a passphrase you choose.

## Your data stays on your phone

Plannr works fully offline and your data stays on your device. Optional features use the internet only when you use them. Android's own Auto Backup may save a copy to your Google account.

There is no account and no sign-in.

## Before you install: back up first

If Plannr is already on your phone with your data:

1. Open Plannr and go to **Data Backup**.
2. Save a backup file, and keep a copy **off the phone** (for example on your computer).
3. Then install the new version over the old one. Your data stays.

**Never uninstall Plannr to make an install work.** Uninstalling deletes all your data on the
phone. If Android says the app cannot be installed, or that it conflicts with the app already on
the phone, stop and keep the old app. Your backup file is your safety copy.

## How to install

1. Download `Plannr.apk` on your Android phone.
2. Open the file. Android may ask you to allow installs from this source (your browser or file
   app). Allow it for this install only if you trust where the file came from.
3. Tap **Install**.

### The Play Protect warning

Plannr is not from the Google Play Store, so Google Play Protect may warn you that it does not
know this app or its developer. This is normal for apps installed from a file. If you trust where
you downloaded it, choose the option to install anyway (the words differ between phones). If you
are not sure, do not install it.
